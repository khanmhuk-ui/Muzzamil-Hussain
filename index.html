About Me

Healthcare Experience Meets Virtual Support

I am a healthcare professional with a nursing background and experience supporting healthcare operations, patients, and clinical administrative workflows.

My experience includes working with patients and healthcare teams while supporting administrative processes such as appointment scheduling, patient communication, insurance verification, prior authorization, and follow-up coordination.

As a Medical Virtual Assistant, I help healthcare providers, clinics, and medical teams manage administrative responsibilities so they can focus more on patient care.

I have experience working with healthcare technology and EHR/EMR platforms, including eClinicalWorks (eCW), DrChrono, DrNotes, Practice Fusion, AthenaHealth, and AltuMed.

My approach combines healthcare knowledge, patient-centered communication, attention to detail, and reliable administrative support.

If your healthcare practice needs dependable support with scheduling, patient communication, EHR/EMR workflows, insurance verification, prior authorization, or other administrative tasks, let's connect.
